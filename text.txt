10. Social Media & Content Platforms
	•	Problem: Detecting bots or fake accounts.
	•	Anomalies:
	•	User posting 500 times in one hour.
	•	Unusual spike in followers overnight.
	•	Action Taken: Block/bot detection system.
	•	Techniques used: Clustering, anomaly scores, graph analysis.

6. Energy Sector
	•	Problem: Detecting energy theft or equipment failure.
	•	Anomalies:
	•	Electricity consumption abnormally low/high.
	•	Sudden voltage spikes in smart meters.
	•	Action Taken: Inspect wiring, prevent energy theft.
	•	Techniques used: Time series anomaly detection (LSTMs, ARIMA).

17. Supply Chain & Logistics
	•	Problem: Tracking unusual shipment/delivery patterns.
	•	Anomalies:
	•	Delivery takes much longer than expected.
	•	Shipment weight doesn’t match invoice.
	•	Action: Investigate theft, damage, or route issues.
	•	Techniques: Time series + anomaly scoring.

2. Customer Reviews & Feedback
	•	Problem: Identifying fake or unusual reviews.
	•	Anomalies:
• Product gets 1,000 five-star reviews in a single day.
• Reviews with repetitive, bot-like text.
	•	Action: Filter suspicious reviews, adjust recommendation scores.
	•	Techniques: NLP embeddings + clustering, One-Class SVM.
